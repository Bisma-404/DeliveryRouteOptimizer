About map and route data

Road geometry and distance calculations use the local Karachi graph. Google Maps and CARTO basemaps require an internet connection. Location autocomplete can query OpenStreetMap Nominatim when local matches are limited.

Map data © Google or © OpenStreetMap contributors and CARTO, depending on the selected layer.
